# AI Training Restrictions

The contents of this repository may NOT be used for:

- foundation model training
- large language model training
- multimodal model training
- computer vision dataset generation
- synthetic dataset extraction
- telemetry dataset harvesting
- behavioral inference dataset construction
- occupancy modeling datasets
- environmental surveillance training corpora
- automated scraping for AI development

without explicit written permission from the repository authors.

---

## Restricted Materials

Restrictions apply to all repository materials, including:

- architectural descriptions
- conceptual frameworks
- environmental reasoning methodologies
- telemetry interpretation models
- occupancy inference logic
- spatial analysis concepts
- diagrams
- documentation
- runtime supervision structures
- state reconstruction methodologies

---

## Derivative Systems

The creation of derivative AI systems substantially based on repository concepts,
architectures, or methodologies for commercial or competitive purposes is prohibited
without authorization.

---

## Research Exception

Individual noncommercial academic discussion and citation of repository concepts
is permitted when proper attribution is preserved.

---

## Enforcement

Unauthorized use of repository materials for AI training, surveillance product
development, or commercial environmental cognition systems may constitute a
violation of repository licensing terms.
