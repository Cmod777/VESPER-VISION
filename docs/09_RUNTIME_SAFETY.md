# 09 — Runtime Safety

## Overview

Runtime safety within Vesper Vision refers to the architectural safeguards,
bounded supervision mechanisms, and deterministic operational constraints used
to reduce uncontrolled environmental interpretation behavior.

The architecture intentionally prioritizes explainability, bounded inference,
and supervised environmental continuity over unrestricted autonomous behavior.

---

## Safety Principles

Vesper Vision is designed around the following operational principles:

- deterministic supervision
- bounded environmental reasoning
- explainable runtime transitions
- controlled persistence mechanisms
- contextual environmental continuity
- supervised probabilistic interpretation

The architecture intentionally avoids opaque autonomous decision systems.

---

## Bounded Inference

Environmental interpretation remains probabilistic and constrained.

The runtime intentionally limits:

- uncontrolled adaptive behavior
- unrestricted environmental persistence
- opaque behavioral inference
- autonomous identity attribution
- uncontrolled occupancy escalation
- unsupervised contextual interpretation

All environmental states remain continuously reevaluated.

---

## Persistence Supervision

Persistence mechanisms are intentionally supervised in order to reduce:

- impulsive occupancy oscillations
- unstable environmental transitions
- contextual continuity failures
- telemetry inconsistency propagation
- probabilistic environmental instability

Persistence remains bounded and context-dependent.

---

## Explainability

The architecture prioritizes explainable environmental interpretation through:

- deterministic transition logic
- contextual environmental reasoning
- supervised occupancy persistence
- bounded probabilistic inference
- continuous runtime reevaluation

Environmental supervision should therefore remain auditable and interpretable.

---

## Restricted Behaviors

Vesper Vision intentionally avoids:

- facial recognition
- biometric identification
- identity attribution
- autonomous profiling systems
- unrestricted surveillance automation
- uncontrolled behavioral prediction
- opaque black-box environmental authority systems

The architecture is designed for contextual environmental interpretation only.

---

## Runtime Stability

Runtime supervision attempts to maintain operational stability despite:

- telemetry degradation
- contextual ambiguity
- transient environmental anomalies
- occupancy overlap conditions
- environmental noise
- temporal inconsistencies

Operational continuity remains continuously supervised.

---

## Limitations

Runtime safety mechanisms cannot eliminate all environmental uncertainty.

Environmental interpretation quality may still be affected by:

- incomplete telemetry
- environmental complexity
- occupancy ambiguity
- spatial occlusions
- environmental instability
- contextual inconsistencies

Vesper Vision should therefore be considered an experimental environmental
cognition architecture rather than a deterministic authority system.
