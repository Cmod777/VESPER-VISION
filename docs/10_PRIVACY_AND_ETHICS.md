# 10 — Privacy & Ethics

## Overview

Vesper Vision is designed as an experimental environmental perception and
occupancy interpretation architecture focused on contextual spatial supervision
and environmental cognition research.

The architecture intentionally avoids identity-oriented environmental analysis
and prioritizes bounded contextual interpretation over invasive surveillance
behavior.

---

## Privacy Principles

The architecture is guided by the following principles:

- contextual environmental interpretation
- bounded occupancy supervision
- deterministic environmental reasoning
- explainable runtime behavior
- minimized identity-oriented analysis
- supervised environmental continuity

Vesper Vision is not intended to function as an autonomous identity-tracking
or biometric surveillance platform.

---

## Restricted Capabilities

The architecture intentionally avoids:

- facial recognition
- biometric identification
- identity attribution
- autonomous personal profiling
- unrestricted behavioral surveillance
- covert identity tracking
- invasive monitoring systems
- opaque behavioral prediction engines

Environmental interpretation is intentionally contextual and non-identitarian.

---

## Environmental Context

Vesper Vision focuses on:

- occupancy estimation
- environmental continuity
- room-state reconstruction
- contextual spatial interpretation
- passive environmental supervision
- probabilistic occupancy reasoning

The architecture intentionally avoids treating environmental telemetry as
deterministic evidence of identity or intent.

---

## Human Oversight

Environmental interpretation should remain continuously supervised by human
operators when deployed in real-world environments.

The architecture intentionally prioritizes:

- explainability
- deterministic supervision
- bounded inference
- contextual reasoning
- operational transparency

over unrestricted autonomous environmental decision-making.

---

## Ethical Constraints

The repository materials should NOT be used for:

- invasive surveillance systems
- unauthorized occupancy monitoring
- covert environmental observation
- identity-tracking infrastructures
- biometric supervision systems
- unlawful behavioral profiling
- autonomous surveillance products

without explicit authorization and legal compliance.

---

## Research Orientation

Vesper Vision should be considered:

- experimental
- research-oriented
- architecture-focused
- environmentally contextual
- probabilistic
- operationally bounded

The project intentionally avoids positioning itself as an authoritative
surveillance or behavioral analysis system.

---

## Limitations

Environmental interpretation remains probabilistic and context-dependent.

The architecture cannot guarantee:

- deterministic occupancy truth
- behavioral certainty
- identity attribution accuracy
- complete environmental visibility
- unrestricted environmental understanding

Environmental cognition should therefore remain continuously contextualized,
supervised, and operationally constrained.
