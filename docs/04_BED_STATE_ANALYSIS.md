# 04 — Bed-State Analysis

## Overview

Bed-state analysis within Vesper Vision refers to the contextual interpretation
of bed occupancy conditions through spatial segmentation, occupancy persistence,
environmental continuity, and multimodal environmental supervision.

The architecture does not treat bed occupancy as a simple binary condition.

Instead, bed-state interpretation is reconstructed through probabilistic spatial
reasoning and contextual occupancy supervision.

---

## Bed Occupancy Concepts

Bed-state analysis may include:

- occupied bed estimation
- bed occupancy persistence
- occupied region reconstruction
- contextual body-presence interpretation
- blanket-covered occupancy estimation
- multi-zone occupancy supervision
- passive spatial occupancy analysis

Environmental interpretation remains contextual and probabilistic.

---

## Spatial Bed Segmentation

The architecture may divide bed environments into multiple logical occupancy
regions in order to estimate:

- occupied sectors
- occupancy distribution
- occupancy continuity
- inactive regions
- contextual spatial arrangement

Segmented interpretation may improve environmental continuity supervision
during partial visibility conditions.

---

## Contextual Occupancy Interpretation

Bed occupancy reasoning may incorporate:

- environmental continuity
- occupancy persistence
- temporal stability
- probabilistic zone activation
- passive motion interpretation
- contextual spatial consistency
- multimodal telemetry fusion

The runtime intentionally avoids impulsive occupancy transitions caused by
short-lived visual anomalies.

---

## Blanket-Covered Occupancy

Environmental interpretation may attempt to estimate occupancy conditions even
when direct body visibility is partially unavailable.

Contextual inference may therefore incorporate:

- spatial deformation persistence
- occupied region continuity
- passive environmental activity
- occupancy stabilization logic
- contextual environmental transitions

These estimations remain probabilistic and environment-dependent.

---

## Runtime Supervision

Bed-state supervision prioritizes:

- deterministic reasoning
- contextual continuity
- explainable transitions
- bounded inference
- adaptive occupancy stabilization
- environmental persistence

The architecture intentionally avoids biometric identification systems and
identity-oriented behavioral analysis.

---

## Limitations

Bed-state analysis accuracy may vary depending on:

- blanket coverage conditions
- environmental lighting
- spatial occlusions
- overlapping occupancy regions
- room geometry
- environmental noise
- contextual ambiguity

Vesper Vision should therefore be considered an experimental environmental
perception architecture rather than a deterministic occupancy authority system.
