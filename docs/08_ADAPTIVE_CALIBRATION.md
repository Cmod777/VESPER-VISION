# 08 — Adaptive Calibration

## Overview

Adaptive calibration within Vesper Vision refers to the supervised adjustment
processes used to maintain environmental interpretation stability across
changing environmental conditions and contextual occupancy scenarios.

The architecture intentionally treats calibration as a bounded and supervised
process rather than an uncontrolled autonomous adaptation mechanism.

---

## Calibration Concepts

Adaptive calibration may include:

- occupancy confidence stabilization
- contextual persistence tuning
- spatial interpretation adjustment
- environmental continuity supervision
- probabilistic transition stabilization
- telemetry consistency adaptation
- contextual occupancy refinement

Calibration behavior remains environment-dependent and runtime-supervised.

---

## Contextual Environmental Adaptation

Environmental conditions may evolve over time due to:

- lighting variability
- room configuration changes
- occupancy distribution changes
- environmental noise
- telemetry inconsistencies
- spatial visibility variations
- contextual environmental transitions

Adaptive supervision attempts to maintain stable environmental interpretation
despite these evolving conditions.

---

## Stateful Calibration

Calibration processes may supervise:

- occupancy persistence thresholds
- environmental continuity behavior
- contextual confidence accumulation
- occupancy decay supervision
- environmental transition sensitivity
- probabilistic environmental stabilization

Calibration adjustments remain bounded and continuously reevaluated.

---

## Deterministic Constraints

Vesper Vision intentionally prioritizes:

- deterministic supervision logic
- explainable adaptive behavior
- bounded environmental reasoning
- controlled calibration adjustments
- contextual environmental stability

The architecture intentionally avoids uncontrolled self-modifying runtime
behavior.

---

## Environmental Stability

Adaptive calibration may assist with:

- reducing impulsive occupancy transitions
- stabilizing contextual interpretation
- maintaining environmental continuity
- supervising telemetry consistency
- contextualizing environmental anomalies
- improving room-state persistence

Environmental interpretation remains probabilistic and continuously supervised.

---

## Runtime Philosophy

Adaptive calibration prioritizes:

- explainable runtime behavior
- bounded environmental adaptation
- contextual supervision
- deterministic operational continuity
- controlled persistence management

The architecture intentionally avoids opaque autonomous optimization systems.

---

## Limitations

Calibration quality may vary depending on:

- telemetry quality
- environmental consistency
- occupancy complexity
- contextual ambiguity
- room geometry
- environmental visibility
- temporal environmental instability

Adaptive calibration should therefore be considered an experimental supervised
environmental stabilization process.
