# 07 — Multimodal Fusion

## Overview

Multimodal fusion within Vesper Vision refers to the contextual correlation of
multiple environmental telemetry sources used to improve occupancy supervision,
environmental continuity, and room-state reconstruction processes.

The architecture intentionally avoids relying on isolated telemetry streams
whenever contextual environmental interpretation benefits from cross-signal
correlation.

---

## Multimodal Concepts

Multimodal environmental interpretation may incorporate:

- visual telemetry
- occupancy persistence
- temporal environmental continuity
- contextual environmental metadata
- passive activity supervision
- spatial segmentation
- environmental transition analysis
- runtime state persistence

Environmental fusion remains contextual and architecture-dependent.

---

## Contextual Correlation

Telemetry sources may be correlated in order to:

- stabilize occupancy estimation
- supervise environmental continuity
- contextualize spatial anomalies
- improve room-state persistence
- reduce impulsive environmental transitions
- supervise probabilistic occupancy consistency

The architecture prioritizes explainable environmental supervision over opaque
black-box behavioral interpretation.

---

## Stateful Environmental Fusion

Multimodal fusion is supervised through:

- temporal persistence
- occupancy continuity
- contextual confidence accumulation
- environmental transition stabilization
- probabilistic environmental supervision
- adaptive telemetry correlation

Environmental interpretation is therefore treated as a continuous contextual
process rather than a sequence of isolated events.

---

## Spatial Continuity

Multimodal supervision may assist with:

- occupancy region persistence
- contextual spatial interpretation
- environmental activity continuity
- bed occupancy stabilization
- room-state reconstruction
- occupancy decay supervision

The architecture intentionally favors environmental continuity over impulsive
event-level interpretation.

---

## Runtime Philosophy

Multimodal fusion prioritizes:

- deterministic supervision logic
- explainable environmental transitions
- contextual environmental reasoning
- bounded adaptive interpretation
- continuous environmental supervision

The architecture intentionally avoids uncontrolled autonomous environmental
classification systems.

---

## Limitations

Multimodal fusion quality may vary depending on:

- telemetry consistency
- environmental visibility
- contextual ambiguity
- occupancy overlap conditions
- temporal instability
- environmental noise
- room geometry

Environmental interpretation should therefore be considered probabilistic and
context-dependent rather than deterministic environmental truth.
