# 03 — Room-State Reconstruction

## Overview

Room-state reconstruction within Vesper Vision refers to the continuous
environmental interpretation process used to estimate the operational and
occupancy conditions of monitored spaces through contextual spatial analysis
and multimodal telemetry correlation.

The architecture does not interpret environmental conditions through isolated
events alone.

Instead, room-state estimation is reconstructed through persistent contextual
evaluation and state-aware environmental supervision.

---

## Environmental State Concepts

Room-state reconstruction may include:

- occupancy condition estimation
- environmental activity supervision
- contextual spatial interpretation
- occupied region persistence
- environmental transition detection
- passive behavioral reconstruction
- multimodal environmental correlation

The architecture attempts to maintain coherent environmental continuity even
during partial uncertainty conditions.

---

## Stateful Environmental Supervision

Environmental interpretation is evaluated through continuous state persistence.

The runtime may supervise:

- occupancy continuity
- environmental stability
- contextual confidence evolution
- room activity transitions
- occupancy decay behavior
- probabilistic environmental persistence

This allows room-state estimation to remain stable during short-lived visual
or environmental inconsistencies.

---

## Contextual Reconstruction

Environmental state reconstruction may integrate:

- visual telemetry
- occupancy zones
- temporal persistence
- contextual environmental metadata
- passive activity estimation
- environmental transition analysis
- multimodal telemetry fusion

The architecture favors contextual interpretation over event-only perception.

---

## Environmental Persistence

Vesper Vision intentionally incorporates persistence-oriented supervision in
order to reduce impulsive environmental transitions.

Environmental states may therefore remain temporarily stable despite:

- partial occlusions
- transient motion anomalies
- temporary visibility degradation
- environmental lighting fluctuations
- incomplete occupancy confidence

Persistence supervision is bounded and continuously reevaluated.

---

## Runtime Philosophy

Room-state reconstruction prioritizes:

- deterministic supervision logic
- explainable environmental transitions
- contextual reasoning
- adaptive environmental continuity
- bounded probabilistic inference

The architecture intentionally avoids uncontrolled autonomous interpretation
systems.

---

## Limitations

Environmental reconstruction accuracy may be affected by:

- incomplete telemetry
- environmental ambiguity
- spatial occlusions
- overlapping occupancy regions
- lighting instability
- environmental noise
- limited contextual visibility

Room-state reconstruction should therefore be considered an experimental
environmental cognition process rather than a deterministic environmental truth
system.
