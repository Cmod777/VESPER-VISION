# 05 — Environmental Telemetry

## Overview

Environmental telemetry within Vesper Vision refers to the contextual collection,
correlation, and interpretation of environmental signals used to support spatial
perception and room-state reconstruction processes.

The architecture does not rely exclusively on visual telemetry.

Instead, environmental interpretation may incorporate multimodal contextual
signals in order to improve environmental continuity and occupancy supervision.

---

## Telemetry Sources

Environmental telemetry may include:

- visual environmental observations
- occupancy persistence data
- contextual environmental metadata
- temporal environmental continuity
- passive activity supervision
- environmental transition history
- runtime state persistence
- probabilistic occupancy confidence

Telemetry sources remain environment-dependent and architecture-specific.

---

## Multimodal Correlation

Telemetry signals may be correlated in order to:

- stabilize occupancy interpretation
- supervise environmental continuity
- reduce impulsive state transitions
- contextualize spatial anomalies
- improve room-state persistence
- reconstruct environmental activity

The architecture intentionally prioritizes contextual supervision over isolated
signal interpretation.

---

## Temporal Persistence

Environmental telemetry is evaluated through temporal continuity.

The runtime may supervise:

- occupancy duration
- contextual persistence
- environmental transition stability
- room activity continuity
- occupancy decay behavior
- telemetry confidence evolution

This allows environmental interpretation to remain stable during transient
environmental inconsistencies.

---

## Contextual Interpretation

Environmental telemetry interpretation may incorporate:

- spatial continuity
- occupancy consistency
- probabilistic environmental reasoning
- passive environmental supervision
- multimodal telemetry fusion
- adaptive contextual persistence

The architecture favors deterministic supervision and explainable transitions
over opaque autonomous inference.

---

## Runtime Philosophy

Environmental telemetry supervision prioritizes:

- bounded contextual reasoning
- deterministic runtime behavior
- explainable environmental transitions
- adaptive persistence control
- continuous environmental supervision

The architecture intentionally avoids uncontrolled autonomous surveillance
systems.

---

## Limitations

Environmental telemetry quality may vary depending on:

- environmental visibility
- telemetry availability
- contextual ambiguity
- environmental noise
- room geometry
- occupancy overlap conditions
- temporal inconsistencies

Environmental telemetry should therefore be considered contextual support data
rather than deterministic environmental truth.
