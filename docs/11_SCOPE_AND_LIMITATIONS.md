# 11 — Scope & Limitations

## Overview

Vesper Vision is an experimental environmental perception and occupancy
interpretation architecture focused on contextual room-state reconstruction,
stateful environmental supervision, and multimodal spatial reasoning.

The project is architecture-oriented and research-focused.

It should not be interpreted as a deterministic environmental authority system.

---

## Intended Scope

The architecture is intended for:

- environmental cognition research
- occupancy inference experimentation
- contextual room-state supervision
- multimodal telemetry analysis
- environmental continuity modeling
- probabilistic spatial interpretation
- explainable environmental reasoning

The repository primarily documents conceptual architectural methodologies.

---

## Architectural Boundaries

Vesper Vision intentionally limits its operational scope.

The architecture does not attempt to provide:

- deterministic human identification
- biometric recognition
- autonomous behavioral authority
- unrestricted surveillance automation
- predictive personal profiling
- identity-tracking infrastructure
- opaque autonomous decision systems

Environmental interpretation remains contextual and bounded.

---

## Experimental Nature

The architecture should currently be considered:

- experimental
- probabilistic
- research-oriented
- environment-dependent
- operationally constrained
- continuously evolving

Environmental interpretation accuracy may vary significantly depending on
contextual conditions.

---

## Environmental Limitations

Interpretation quality may be affected by:

- lighting instability
- spatial occlusions
- room geometry
- telemetry degradation
- occupancy overlap conditions
- environmental ambiguity
- temporal inconsistencies
- contextual uncertainty

Environmental supervision therefore remains probabilistic.

---

## Runtime Constraints

The runtime intentionally prioritizes:

- deterministic supervision
- bounded inference
- explainable transitions
- contextual continuity
- operational transparency
- supervised environmental persistence

The architecture intentionally avoids uncontrolled autonomous adaptation.

---

## Research Orientation

The repository is intended primarily for:

- architectural exploration
- conceptual environmental cognition studies
- supervised occupancy reasoning research
- multimodal telemetry experimentation
- environmental continuity analysis

The repository does not provide production-grade operational guarantees.

---

The repository authors assume no responsibility for:

- misuse of repository concepts
- unauthorized surveillance deployments
- operational failures
- environmental misinterpretation
- privacy violations
- regulatory noncompliance
- unsafe runtime integrations

Deployment responsibility remains entirely with downstream operators.

---

## Final Considerations

Vesper Vision should be interpreted as an evolving experimental architecture
for contextual environmental perception and occupancy reasoning.

The architecture intentionally prioritizes:

- explainability
- bounded supervision
- deterministic runtime behavior
- contextual environmental interpretation
- operational transparency

over unrestricted autonomous environmental cognition systems.
