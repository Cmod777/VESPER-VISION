# 06 — Runtime Supervision

## Overview

Runtime supervision within Vesper Vision refers to the continuous orchestration,
state evaluation, and environmental continuity management processes responsible
for maintaining stable environmental interpretation across the architecture.

The runtime is designed around stateful supervision principles rather than
event-only environmental reactions.

Environmental interpretation is therefore continuously reevaluated through
contextual persistence and probabilistic environmental continuity.

---

## Stateful Runtime Model

The runtime architecture supervises environmental interpretation through:

- occupancy persistence
- contextual continuity
- environmental confidence stabilization
- temporal state supervision
- adaptive transition evaluation
- bounded probabilistic reasoning

Environmental conditions are intentionally interpreted as evolving contextual
states rather than isolated detections.

---

## Continuous Supervision

The runtime may continuously supervise:

- occupancy conditions
- room-state persistence
- environmental transitions
- occupancy decay behavior
- telemetry continuity
- contextual environmental stability
- probabilistic environmental consistency

Continuous supervision attempts to reduce impulsive state oscillations caused
by transient environmental anomalies.

---

## Transition Management

Environmental transitions may be evaluated through:

- temporal persistence
- occupancy continuity
- confidence evolution
- contextual environmental stability
- multimodal telemetry correlation
- bounded adaptive supervision

Transitions remain probabilistic and continuously reevaluated.

---

## Deterministic Supervision

Vesper Vision intentionally prioritizes:

- deterministic runtime logic
- explainable state transitions
- bounded contextual reasoning
- supervised environmental continuity
- controlled adaptive interpretation

The architecture intentionally avoids uncontrolled autonomous environmental
decision systems.

---

## Runtime Persistence

Runtime supervision may maintain environmental continuity despite:

- partial telemetry degradation
- temporary visual inconsistencies
- environmental noise
- transient occupancy ambiguity
- short-lived spatial anomalies

Persistence mechanisms remain bounded and continuously supervised.

---

## Runtime Safety

The runtime architecture prioritizes:

- explainable supervision behavior
- bounded environmental inference
- controlled persistence logic
- contextual stability
- deterministic operational behavior

The architecture intentionally avoids opaque autonomous surveillance behavior.

---

## Limitations

Runtime supervision quality may vary depending on:

- telemetry completeness
- environmental consistency
- contextual ambiguity
- occupancy overlap conditions
- environmental noise
- room geometry
- temporal instability

Runtime supervision should therefore be considered an experimental
environmental cognition process rather than a deterministic authority system.
