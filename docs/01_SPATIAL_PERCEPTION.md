# 01 — Spatial Perception

## Overview

Spatial perception within Vesper Vision refers to the continuous interpretation
of environmental spatial conditions through contextual visual telemetry and
stateful environmental reasoning.

The architecture does not rely exclusively on isolated object detection events.

Instead, environmental interpretation is reconstructed through:

- contextual occupancy analysis
- spatial continuity
- environmental persistence
- probabilistic zone interpretation
- multimodal telemetry correlation
- temporal state supervision

---

## Environmental Context

Spatial interpretation may include:

- room occupancy estimation
- occupied zone reconstruction
- bed-state interpretation
- contextual motion analysis
- environmental activity supervision
- passive spatial monitoring

The architecture prioritizes explainable environmental reasoning over opaque
black-box classification systems.

---

## Stateful Interpretation

Vesper Vision treats environmental perception as a continuous stateful process.

Environmental conditions are evaluated through:

- temporal continuity
- contextual persistence
- confidence accumulation
- environmental transitions
- occupancy stability
- adaptive spatial interpretation

rather than isolated frame-level detections.

---

## Spatial Segmentation

Environmental spaces may be divided into logical occupancy regions in order to:

- estimate occupied environmental sectors
- distinguish active and inactive areas
- reconstruct occupancy distribution
- estimate spatial behavioral organization
- supervise bed occupancy zones

Spatial segmentation remains contextual and environment-dependent.

---

## Runtime Philosophy

The architecture intentionally favors:

- deterministic supervision
- bounded inference
- explainable state transitions
- contextual interpretation
- environmental continuity

over fully autonomous surveillance-oriented systems.
